Who we are:

At Frontier Transport, we provide reliable West Coast trucking and freight transportation services. Based in the Tri-Cities of Washington, we specialize in hauling meats and handling a wide range of freight with our secure dry and cold storage solutions.

With two key locations, a warehouse in Pasco, WA and a truck yard in Burbank, WA, our operation is built for speed and efficiency. Shipments are staged and stored in Pasco, where our warehouse team carefully prepares and loads trailers. From there, our drivers head out of Burbank to destinations across Northern California, Southern California, and Arizona. On the return, we secure backhaul loads, maximizing efficiency while delivering dependable service both ways.

Our team of 20 + dedicated employees include skilled truck drivers, warehouse professionals, an experienced mechanic, and a supportive office staff. Together, we combine professionalism with a personal touch, offering safe, on time, and cost-effective transportation and storage services that our partners can trust.

At Frontier Transport, we are committed to reliability, safety, and efficiency, making sure every shipment arrives on time, every mile of the way.

Our Process:

Loads stored & prepped in Pasco.

Trailers loaded & ready.

Drivers pick-up trucks/trailers in Burbank.

Delivery to destination.

Backhaul secured & return trip.
